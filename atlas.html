Commands

Powerful commands,
intuitive syntax

Every command designed for speed. Slash commands with autocomplete mean your mods spend less time typing and more time moderating.

/banBan a user with optional reason, duration, and message delete window. Supports multiple users at once.
/timeoutTemporarily restrict a member with human-readable durations like "2h", "3d", "1w".
/warnIssue formal warnings that trigger auto-escalation after configurable thresholds.
/caseLook up any user's full moderation history, case count, and trend analysis at a glance.
/purgeBulk-delete messages with filters: by user, content regex, attachments, or bots only.
/lockdownInstantly lock all channels or targeted categories during raids with one command.
